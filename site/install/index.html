INFILTRATE YOUR FM-1

This page puts EVILGENIUS on an M-VAVE FM-1 over its USB cable. It needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web MIDI); nothing is installed on the computer.

This browser cannot talk to the FM-1. It has no Web MIDI. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1 BEFORE YOU START

  • Back up first. EVILGENIUS keeps part of itself in the flash at 0x97000 to 0xAEFFF, where other firmwares keep projects, presets or samples. Installing it overwrites that data. Save it with the firmware you have now before you go on.
  • Use a USB data cable, straight to the computer. Close other tabs and apps that use MIDI (another firmware's installer or editor, a DAW).
  • Coming from M-VAVE's own firmware or from another custom firmware both work. M-VAVE's "Open" firmware does not: put the stock firmware back first (below).
  • Do not unplug while it writes. If it is cut off, plug it back in and press Install again: it carries on.

GOING BACK

To return to M-VAVE's firmware, download its FM-1.fwsc from M-VAVE, choose it here and install it. Your EVILGENIUS projects and presets stay in the flash but M-VAVE's firmware does not read them: back them up in the manager first.
